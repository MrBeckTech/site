Scientific Notation Practice

SCIENCE 1206 · UNIT 3

Write numbers in scientific notation and back again, and type them on a calculator with the EE key.

IN PLAIN WORDS

Scientific notation is a short way to write huge or tiny numbers: the number, then how many places the decimal moved. 47 000 m is 4.7 × 104 m: write 4.7, then move the decimal point 4 places.

It is also how you show sig figs clearly: 100 cm with 3 sig figs is written 1.00 × 102 cm.

MODE
STEP BY STEP

SCIENTIFIC NOTATION

SCIENTIFIC NOTATIONEXAMPLE
Writes a number as a coefficient between 1 and 10, multiplied by a power of 10. Positive exponent → number 10 or bigger. Negative exponent → number smaller than 1. Exponent 0 → number from 1 to 10. 300 000 000 = 3 × 108
0.000 000 03 = 3 × 10−8
Useful for very large or very small numbers, and for showing the correct number of significant figures. 100 cm to 3 SF → 1.00 × 102 cm
100 cm to 2 SF → 1.0 × 102 cm
100 cm to 1 SF → 1 × 102 cm
4.7 × 104
  • Coefficient (4.7): 1 ≤ number < 10. All its digits are significant.
  • Exponent (4): how many places the decimal point moved.

Watch out: 47 × 103 and 0.47 × 105 are NOT proper scientific notation. The coefficient must be at least 1 and less than 10.

HOW TO CONVERT, STEP BY STEP

Big number: 3200 → 2 SF

  1. Put the decimal after the first non-zero digit: 3.200
  2. Count the places the decimal moved: 3200. → 3.200 = 3 places left
  3. Moved left (big number) → positive exponent: 3.2 × 103

Small number: 0.000855 → 2 SF

  1. Put the decimal after the first non-zero digit: 8.55
  2. Count the places: 0.000855 → 8.55 = 4 places right
  3. Moved right (small number) → negative: 8.55 × 10−4 → 2 SF: 8.6 × 10−4

THE CALCULATOR: EE KEY

Use the EE (or ×10x) key: 3.2 × 103 is typed 3.2 EE 3. This keeps the whole number together.

If you type 6 ÷ 2 × 10 ^ 3 instead of 6 ÷ 2 EE 3, the calculator does 6 ÷ 2 first and then multiplies by 1000, giving 3000 instead of 0.003.

For a negative exponent, press the negative key (−) before the exponent: 8.6 EE (−) 4.
