IN PLAIN WORDS

Unit conversions multiply by a fraction that equals 1, so the amount stays the same and only the unit changes. 1 km and 1000 m are the same distance, so 1 km over 1000 m is just 1.

The conversion numbers (1000, 100, 60, 3600, 3.6) are exact, so the answer keeps the sig figs of the measurement you started with.

In physics we use the SI system (International System of Units). The SI unit for distance is the metre (m), for time the second (s), and for speed metres per second (m/s). Most formulas work best when everything is in SI units.

Base unit: a basic unit that all other units are built from, like the metre (m) and the second (s). Derived unit: a unit made by combining base units: m/s is metres ÷ seconds. km, cm and mm are not new units: they are the metre with a prefix (kilo = 1000, centi = 1/100, milli = 1/1000).

CONVERSIONS TO KNOW

Distance: 1 cm = 10 mm · 1 m = 100 cm · 1 km = 1000 m

km× 1000 →m× 100 →cm× 10 →mm

Time: 1 min = 60 s · 1 h = 60 min · 1 h = 3600 s

Speed: km/h → m/s: ÷ 3.6 · m/s → km/h: × 3.6

Big unit → small unit: multiply (you need MORE of the small unit). Small unit → big unit: divide.

All of these are exact, so they never limit sig figs.

UNIT ANALYSIS: HOW IT WORKS

A conversion fraction equals 1 because its top and bottom are the same length (1 km and 1000 m are the same distance). Multiplying by 1 changes the unit, not the amount.

  1. Write the measurement you are given, with its unit.
  2. Multiply by a fraction with the unit you want to get rid of on the bottom and the unit you want on top.
  3. Cancel the unit that is on the top and the bottom. Then multiply the tops and divide by the bottoms.
  4. Check: does it make sense? km is a bigger unit than m, so the number in km should be smaller.

WHERE DOES 3.6 COME FROM?

Convert 1 km/h to m/s with unit analysis:

1 km/h is the same as 1 ÷ 3.6 m/s, so km/h → m/s means divide by 3.6. Sanity check: the m/s number is always smaller than the km/h number. A highway speed of 100 km/h is only about 28 m/s. If your m/s answer is bigger, you multiplied when you should have divided.

SIG FIGS IN CONVERSIONS

Sig figs don't change in a conversion, because 100 and 1000 are exact: 381 m (3 SF) = 38 100 cm = 3.81 × 104 cm (still 3 SF).

Round only at the end: 1.4 h × 60 × 60 = 5040 s. 1.4 has 2 SF → 5.0 × 103 s.

Watch out: 12.5 m/s × 3.6 = 45 on the calculator, but 12.5 has 3 SF, so the answer must show 3 SF: 45.0 km/h. The zero is not extra; it tells the reader how precise the answer is.
